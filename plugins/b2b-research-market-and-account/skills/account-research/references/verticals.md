# The nine verticals: buying roles and where accounts disclose what matters

Use this to pick the roles, fields and public places to look for each account's technology, organisation and situation. It carries no statistic, benchmark or fact about a named company. Every account fact comes from a page fetched in the session, the user's connector or the user's own file.

| Vertical | Roles that usually buy or influence | Where technographics tend to be public | Where situation signals tend to be public | Cautions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logistics tech | heads of logistics, supply chain, transport, operations, IT | integration and partner pages, careers pages, customer case studies | results releases, network or warehouse expansion releases, contract wins announced by the account | a carrier, a shipper and a software vendor are different accounts with different buyers |
| Fintech | CFO, finance controller, treasury, risk, compliance, IT | developer documentation, integration pages, regulator filings | regulator approvals and licences, results, product launches | regulated; quote the regulator's own page; no advice |
| SaaS | functional heads, IT, operations, product | integration marketplaces, developer docs, careers pages | funding and results releases, launches, new regions | do not assume self serve buying for a product sold by sales teams |
| Vertical SaaS | operators of the industry served plus IT; at the vendor, product and engineering heads | integration lists, marketplace and partner pages | results, new modules, new markets, large customer wins announced by the account | use the industry's own words for its workflows |
| AI native companies | functional heads, IT, security, data leaders | model and platform partner pages, developer docs, trust and security pages | launches, evaluations published with method, funding | no capability claim without the account's own page or a published evaluation |
| ITeS (IT and IT-enabled services) | CIO, CTO, delivery and engineering heads, procurement, global capability centre leaders | partner and alliance pages, certifications, careers pages | deal wins announced by the account, results, new centres | services are bought through relationships and proposals |
| Telecom | enterprise IT and network heads; at operators, network and operations heads | regulator filings, network and partner announcements | spectrum or licence events, results, enterprise product launches | say which telecom business the account is in |
| Software companies | engineering, IT, product leaders, security reviewers | release notes, documentation, marketplace listings, careers pages | releases, results, acquisitions announced by the account | separate shipped from roadmap |
| Cybersecurity | CISO, security operations, risk, compliance, IT | trust and security pages, certifications, partner pages | published research by the account, product launches, results | never speculate about a breach or name a victim without a public source; no fear selling |

Company stages these notes were written for: pre Series A through Series C. Regions: India, Middle East and Africa, Southeast Asia, United Kingdom, United States, Western Europe. For any other sector, work only from what the user typed and pages fetched in the session.
